# GAIN (Digitone effect)

The smallest effect on fxkit ([../../mods/fxkit-dn1](../../mods/fxkit-dn1)):
a level, a tone and the right side's phase on the Digitone mk1's inputs.
GAIN in the Mod Menu (hold a track key) makes it the active effect and
opens its page: SYN1 its knobs (GAIN, TONE, PHAS), AMP fxkit's MIXER, LFO its
two LFOs (LFO again for LFO 2), RECORD the LOCK mode (hold a trig, turn a
knob: that step's p-lock; FUNC + trig clears it), MIDI the stock UI for the
MIDI tracks while GAIN plays (its entry brings the page back), NO GAIN off.
Its knobs, mixer and LFOs are saved with the project, its locks with the
pattern. It names no firmware address, so its OS 1.44 port is empty. Needs
core-dn1 4.1 and fxkit.
